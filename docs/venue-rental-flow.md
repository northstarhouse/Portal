# How a Venue Rental Moves Through the Portal

A guide for staff and volunteers. It follows one booking from first inquiry to after the event. Most emails below go out automatically, so your job at each step is to check, not to send.

**Where to work:** Portal → Venue Rentals. Tabs: Inquiries, Clients, Messages.

---

## 1. Inquiry
Someone fills out the Wedding Inquiry or Event Inquiry form on the website.
- They appear under **Inquiries**.
- **Automatic:** they get our welcome guide and a link to book a tour.
- **If they don't book a tour:** a follow-up goes out after 2 days, a final one after 30 days, and after 5 more days they're archived.
- **You:** read their answers, and reply to anything personal under **Messages**.

## 2. Tour
They book a tour from the link (or you book it for them).
- **Automatic:** a confirmation email, a reminder 24 hours before, and another 1 hour before.
- **You:** give the tour. The tour list shows who has booked or requested one.

## 3. Pre-Booking Form
About an hour after the tour, they are emailed the **Pre-Booking Form**. Weddings and other events use different versions.
- Once they submit it, they become a **Client** with their own booking record. Check the event date, because it becomes their venue code.
- For a group that never toured (a nonprofit or special arrangement), use **Send the pre-booking form by hand** on the Inquiries tab.

## 4. Agreement
The rental agreement is sent for signature.
- Everyone required has to sign. You can see who has signed in the client's file.
- **You:** watch for unsigned agreements and nudge the client if it stalls.

## 5. Payments
The client's **payment schedule** is built automatically. For a wedding the rental is $5,000, split into:
- **Initial Payment** (due at booking),
- **Refundable Security Deposit** ($800, due closest to the event),
- Two more installments.

Clients pay online with PayPal on their **event page** at venue.northstarhouse.org, using their **venue code**. The code is the event date, for example `OCT-03-2027`.
- Online payments are recorded automatically and mark the schedule paid.
- **Checks, cash, or anything paid another way:** use **Record a payment** on the client's file.
- **If a client says they paid but it doesn't show:** do not ask them to pay again. Check PayPal for the payment and tell the admin, who will record it against the booking.

### Private event rates (non-wedding)
| Day | Rate |
|---|---|
| Saturday | $1,200 |
| Sunday | $1,000 |
| Weekdays | $800 |

Each rental is 6 hours on site, between 10 AM and 10 PM. An extra hour is $150. Dates can be booked up to 8 months ahead. Memorial and nonprofit events have their own rates.

## 6. What the client does on their event page
- Pays their balance.
- Uploads their **certificate of insurance** (filed in Google Drive).
- Fills out the **Event Day Questionnaire** (timeline, tables and chairs, vendors, day-of contacts).
- Chooses their **brick wording** (weddings; 3 lines, up to 20 characters each).
- Books a **second walk-through**.
- Sends us a message ("Message our team"), which shows up under **Messages**.

## 7. Before the event
- **60 days before:** reminder emails go out for the questionnaire, insurance, and brick wording.
- **30 days before:** a second reminder if the questionnaire still isn't done.
- **You:** use the client file to confirm insurance, payments, and questionnaire are all complete. Chase anything missing.

## 8. After the event
- The booking is marked **Completed** the day after the event.
- **7 days later:** the client is emailed a feedback questionnaire.
- **30 days after that**, with no response, the booking is archived.

---

## Handling messages
Under **Messages**:
1. Read what the client sent.
2. Choose who is handling it (Jen, Derek or Haley).
3. Type your reply and click **Send reply & mark handled**, or **Mark handled without replying**.
4. Use **Save draft** to keep an unfinished reply or notes for later. Notes are saved for everyone, but reply drafts only stay on your own computer.

## Quick checks for any client
- Is the agreement signed?
- Does the payment schedule match what they've actually paid?
- Is insurance uploaded?
- Is the questionnaire done?
- Are there unanswered messages?
