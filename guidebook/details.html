Venue & Rental Agreement Details

Setup, Rentals & Planning

Event Timing

Your rental period is 10 hours, beginning when we open the indoor venue for you. You may choose your preferred start time, with the latest possible end time being 11 PM. Should you require additional time, extra hours are available for $150 per hour.

Setup & Breakdown

All setup and breakdown must occur within your contracted rental time. Outdoor setup is permitted before your rental period begins, but please note that other routine activities (e.g., tours, grounds maintenance) may still take place. If you plan to arrive early for outdoor setup, please let us know in advance so we can coordinate accordingly.

Catering

We require professional caterers for weddings to handle food service, rental breakdown, and trash cleanup to ensure a smooth experience and protect your security deposit. If your chosen caterer has not worked with us before, we’ll coordinate with them to familiarize them with our facilities. For inspiration, you can explore our recommended vendor list below.

Available Rentals

We’re pleased to provide the following rentals for your event: 25 round 60” tables, 200 white folding chairs, 3 rectangular 8’ tables, 2 rectangular 6’ tables, and patio tables, chairs, and umbrellas. Feel free to use any or all of these, or bring in additional rentals. We kindly ask that all furniture, including ours, is returned to its original location before your departure.

Rental Company

If you’re using a rental company, please coordinate delivery for the day of your event and pickup by 10 AM the following morning. We cannot accommodate deliveries the day before if there is another event scheduled. If you encounter any scheduling challenges, please have your rental provider contact us directly to ensure a smooth process.

Weather & Tenting

To prepare for any weather conditions, we recommend having a backup plan that includes tenting options. Tents can be set up in the courtyard or other areas of the property, and we require the use of weighted anchors or sandbags rather than stakes. We will work directly with your tent supplier to ensure everything is properly coordinated.

Event Insurance

Event insurance is required and due one month before your wedding. You are welcome to use any provider of your choice. For ease, EventHelper offers pre-filled coverage requirements. Simply enter your event details to get started.

Planning Support

We are here to assist you every step of the way! We offer up to two onsite planning meetings to review your event details, answer questions, and provide expert feedback. Approximately 60 days before your wedding, we will send you a questionnaire to gather the final details for our Site Manager. Returning this promptly allows us to finalize logistics and ensures a smooth planning process, which we will discuss in a meeting or call.

Lighting

The estate is beautifully illuminated with string lights in the courtyard and over the East Lawn, twinkle pillar lights, and trim lighting on the House, all designed to create a magical atmosphere for your event.
