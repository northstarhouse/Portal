Welcome to North Star House

Designed by Julia Morgan, Restored by You

We’re honored that you’ve chosen to celebrate your wedding at North Star House – a place where natural beauty, rich history, and community spirit come together to create something truly unforgettable.

Nestled in the Sierra Foothills and designed in 1905 by the legendary Julia Morgan, our historic estate is more than a beautiful venue: it’s a living piece of California’s heritage. When you say your vows here, you become part of a long story of resilience, romance, and restoration.

This guidebook is your companion through the planning process. Inside, you’ll find all the information you need to bring your vision to life: from logistics and policies to trusted vendors and thoughtful planning tips.

As you plan your celebration, know that every detail – every toast, dance, and shared moment – adds to the magic of this place. And every event held here helps preserve the House for future generations.

We can’t wait to see your story unfold.
