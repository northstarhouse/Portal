Welcome to North Star House

Designed by Julia Morgan, Restored by You

We’re honored that you’ve chosen to celebrate your wedding at North Star House – a place where natural beauty, rich history, and community spirit come together to create something truly unforgettable.

Nestled in the Sierra Foothills and designed in 1905 by the legendary Julia Morgan, our historic estate is more than a beautiful venue: it’s a living piece of California’s heritage. When you say your vows here, you become part of a long story of resilience, romance, and restoration.

This guidebook is your companion through the planning process. Inside, you’ll find all the information you need to bring your vision to life: from logistics and policies to trusted vendors and thoughtful planning tips.

As you plan your celebration, know that every detail – every toast, dance, and shared moment – adds to the magic of this place. And every event held here helps preserve the House for future generations.

We can’t wait to see your story unfold.

The Spaces

About

The West Lawn

Our most popular ceremony location, the West Lawn offers a stunning natural setting framed by mature trees and the timeless architecture of the historic North Star House. This expansive lawn provides ample space and flexibility for a variety of ceremony layouts, whether you’re planning an intimate gathering or a larger celebration. The surrounding landscape creates a serene and picturesque backdrop, perfect for wedding portraits and candid moments alike.

About

The East Lawn

The East Lawn is one of the largest and most versatile outdoor spaces at the North Star House, often used for receptions, dinners, and community events. Located just outside the catering kitchen, it allows for easy vendor access and smooth food service.

The flat, open layout is ideal for accommodating tables, tents, and decor, all framed by mature trees and the historic stone facade of the house. The nearby Courtyard is typically used for dancing, creating a natural flow between dinner and entertainment. With power access and convenient entry points, the East Lawn is a practical yet picturesque choice for outdoor gatherings.

About

Heritage Garden

Tucked behind the House, the Heritage Garden is a peaceful space filled with native plants, historic charm, and natural beauty. Couples often choose it for portraits or first-look photos, taking advantage of the garden’s rich textures and seasonal blooms. Whether it’s a quiet moment alone, a scenic backdrop, or simply a beautiful place for your guests to explore, the Heritage Garden adds a timeless touch to your wedding day story.

About

Catering Kitchen

Our catering kitchen is located near the Courtyard, just off the East Lawn – perfectly positioned for receptions and events in that area. The space includes ample prep counters, refrigeration, sinks, and easy access for loading and unloading. While not a full commercial kitchen, it is designed to support staging, plating, and service for your event. We recommend coordinating directly with your caterer to ensure the space meets their needs.

About

The Courtyard

The Courtyard is a favorite outdoor space located just off the East Lawn and next to the catering kitchen. Framed by historic stone walls and the architectural character of the House, it’s commonly used for first dances, DJ or band setups, cocktail hour, and evening receptions. The area includes power access and accommodates lighting and sound equipment. Its intimate layout and open-air atmosphere make it an ideal setting for celebration.

About

The Terrace

The Terrace, located along the south side of the House, is a scenic space often used for cocktail hours, guest mingling, and sunset views. With its historic stonework and natural slope overlooking the grounds, it offers a relaxed yet elegant atmosphere. The area is ideal for setting up lounge furniture or high-top tables and serves as a seamless transition space between indoor and outdoor festivities.

About

Reception Hall & Dining Room

The Reception Hall and Dining Room provide an elegant indoor option for events at the North Star House. These connected spaces feature original woodwork, large windows, and historic details that create a warm, welcoming atmosphere. Commonly used for indoor receptions, dinners, or smaller gatherings, the combined area can comfortably accommodate up to 76 guests. This is a great option for events during colder months or for clients looking for an intimate setting within the historic home itself.

The layout allows for flexibility in table arrangements, and guests can easily transition between the indoor spaces and the nearby terrace and courtyard.

Venue & Rental Agreement Details

Setup, Rentals & Planning

Event Timing

Your rental period is 10 hours, beginning when we open the indoor venue for you. You may choose your preferred start time, with the latest possible end time being 11 PM. Should you require additional time, extra hours are available for $150 per hour.

Setup & Breakdown

All setup and breakdown must occur within your contracted rental time. Outdoor setup is permitted before your rental period begins, but please note that other routine activities (e.g., tours, grounds maintenance) may still take place. If you plan to arrive early for outdoor setup, please let us know in advance so we can coordinate accordingly.

Catering

We require professional caterers for weddings to handle food service, rental breakdown, and trash cleanup to ensure a smooth experience and protect your security deposit. If your chosen caterer has not worked with us before, we’ll coordinate with them to familiarize them with our facilities. For inspiration, you can explore our recommended vendor list below.

Available Rentals

We’re pleased to provide the following rentals for your event: 25 round 60” tables, 200 white folding chairs, 3 rectangular 8’ tables, 2 rectangular 6’ tables, and patio tables, chairs, and umbrellas. Feel free to use any or all of these, or bring in additional rentals. We kindly ask that all furniture, including ours, is returned to its original location before your departure.

Rental Company

If you’re using a rental company, please coordinate delivery for the day of your event and pickup by 10 AM the following morning. We cannot accommodate deliveries the day before if there is another event scheduled. If you encounter any scheduling challenges, please have your rental provider contact us directly to ensure a smooth process.

Weather & Tenting

To prepare for any weather conditions, we recommend having a backup plan that includes tenting options. Tents can be set up in the courtyard or other areas of the property, and we require the use of weighted anchors or sandbags rather than stakes. We will work directly with your tent supplier to ensure everything is properly coordinated.

Event Insurance

Event insurance is required and due one month before your wedding. You are welcome to use any provider of your choice. For ease, EventHelper offers pre-filled coverage requirements. Simply enter your event details to get started.

Planning Support

We are here to assist you every step of the way! We offer up to two onsite planning meetings to review your event details, answer questions, and provide expert feedback. Approximately 60 days before your wedding, we will send you a questionnaire to gather the final details for our Site Manager. Returning this promptly allows us to finalize logistics and ensures a smooth planning process, which we will discuss in a meeting or call.

Lighting

The estate is beautifully illuminated with string lights in the courtyard and over the East Lawn, twinkle pillar lights, and trim lighting on the House, all designed to create a magical atmosphere for your event.

Site Managers

A Site Manager will be assigned to your wedding day by your venue coordinators and will be on-site to ensure the venue is clean, prepared, and functioning as expected. They are there to support you and your team with anything related to the venue itself, including restroom supplies, lighting access, and any last-minute property questions.

They will also help ensure your caterer and coordinator are aware of venue expectations by providing them with a copy of the Client Rental Responsibilities Checklist on the day of your event. This helps confirm that key venue guidelines, cleanup expectations, and property-use requirements have been clearly communicated to the professionals supporting your event.

While your Site Manager is there to assist with venue operations and property-related needs, they do not take the place of a wedding coordinator or planner. Their role is specifically to represent the venue, support venue functionality, and help ensure that all venue-related standards and agreements are upheld throughout your wedding day.
