Payment Schedule Overview

Your Payment Plan

To help make the planning process smooth and manageable, we’ve broken your payment plan into clear, timed installments:

  • RetainerDue upon booking to reserve your date
  • First InstallmentDue 5 months before your event
  • Second InstallmentDue 3 months before your event
  • Security DepositDue 1 month before your event

All payment dates and amounts are listed in your invoice. We’ll email you a secure payment link for each payment, along with reminders to keep you on track.

Wedding Details Questionnaire

Your Final Details

About 60 days before your event, we will send you a detailed questionnaire to help us gather the final logistics and ensure everything is coordinated for your special day. This questionnaire will cover essential event details, including timelines, vendor information, and any special requests you may have. Completing and returning the questionnaire promptly will allow us to finalize everything and ensure a seamless event experience.
